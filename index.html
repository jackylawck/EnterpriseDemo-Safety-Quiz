📌 專案摘要與合規定位 📌 Executive Summary & Compliance Positioning

本儲存庫為一項企業管治示範專案，展示如何以輕量級 Streamlit 介面、動態 Secrets 隔離與確定性「治理即代碼」（Governance-as-Code）構建地盤入職安全訓練評估閉環。

This repository is an Enterprise Demo demonstrating a Zero-Data-Retention safety onboarding assessment and archiving workflow built with Streamlit, isolated Cloud Secrets, and deterministic Governance-as-Code.

傳統地盤紙本入職安全考核常面臨字跡難以辨識、缺乏標準時間戳、紙本易遺失及審計調閱耗時等風險。EnterpriseDemo-Safety-Quiz 針對香港法定職安健要求（第 59 章《工廠及工業經營條例》）及國際管理體系（ISO 45001 / ISO 9001 / ISO/IEC 27001）設計，在不架設雲端資料庫、零個人資料持久化（Zero-Data-Retention）的前提下，讓新進工友即時完成安全測驗並自動生成受控 PDF 報告（DEMO-SED-REC-001），一鍵透過後端 SMTP 直送安全及環保組（SED）郵箱，亦可無縫銜接下游 Microsoft 365 SharePoint 自動化歸檔藍圖。

Paper-based construction site safety induction tests suffer from illegible records, missing timestamps, loss of forms, and manual filing overhead. EnterpriseDemo-Safety-Quiz aligns with Hong Kong occupational safety requirements (FIUO Cap. 59) and international standards (ISO 45001 / ISO 9001 / ISO/IEC 27001). Without provisioning any external database, worker inputs exist strictly in volatile session memory to render a controlled PDF record (DEMO-SED-REC-001) and dispatch it via encrypted SMTP to the Safety & Environmental Department (SED), ready for downstream M365 SharePoint auto-archiving.

在法規邊界定性上，本系統完全採用確定性單選比對邏輯（Deterministic Rule-Based Logic），不含任何 AI / 機器學習模型或自主演算法，明確排除於《歐盟人工智能法案》（EU AI Act）及 ISO/IEC 42001 適用範圍之外，同時全面對齊香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》（第 486 章）保障資料原則（DPP 1–4）。

From a regulatory boundary perspective, this application relies strictly on deterministic exact-match grading logic (Non-AI) with zero machine learning or autonomous decision models—explicitly scoping out EU AI Act and ISO/IEC 42001 obligations while adhering to Hong Kong PDPO (Cap. 486) Data Protection Principles (DPP 1–4).

⚙️ 技術亮點與合規工程實踐 ⚙️ Technical & Compliance Engineering Highlights

1. 零資料留存架構Zero-Data-Retention

無外置資料庫或雲端檔案桶，個人資料僅於記憶體暫存用於單次 PDF 渲染，完畢立即釋放，從根源杜絕資料庫外洩。No external DB or storage bucket; PII resides strictly in session RAM for one-time PDF rendering before automatic purge.

2. 動態 Secrets 機密隔離Dynamic Secrets Isolation

題庫答案、門禁通行碼與 SMTP 憑證均抽離至雲端環境變數保管，公開倉庫 100% 零機密暴露，支援免重部署更新試題。Quiz keys, access codes, and SMTP credentials live solely in Cloud Secrets—zero repo exposure with hot-swappable quiz banks.

3. 輸入消毒與檔名防護Input & Filename Sanitization

強制限制欄位長度並以正則過濾換行及 \/*?:"<>| 等危險字元，防範注入與路徑穿越（Path Traversal）攻擊。Enforces strict character caps and regex sanitization of control/path characters to block injection and traversal attacks.

4. 香港時區標準化存證HKT Timezone Normalization

底層統一鎖定 UTC+8 香港標準時間（HKT）蓋印 PDF 報告與審計紀錄，確保完全符合本地法定安全稽核查驗要求。Standardizes all PDF timestamps to Hong Kong Time (UTC+8 HKT) to guarantee consistent legal and IMS audit traceability.

5. 確定性非 AI 治理邊界Non-AI Regulatory Scope-Out

刻意採用透明確定性規則比對，不含任何 AI/ML 演算法，主動排除 EU AI Act 及 ISO/IEC 42001 監管合規負擔。Deliberately utilizes transparent rule-based matching with zero AI/ML models, cleanly scoping out EU AI Act and ISO 42001.

6. 下游 M365 自動歸檔藍圖Downstream M365 Blueprint

附帶 JSON Schema 校驗規範與 Power Automate 藍圖，可零額外授權將 SMTP 郵件附件自動轉存至受控 SharePoint 文件庫。Ships with JSON Schemas and a Standard-Connector Power Automate blueprint to auto-archive emailed PDFs into SharePoint.
